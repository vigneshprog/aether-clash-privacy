Privacy Policy for Aether Clash 3D

Effective Date: March 2025

Aether Clash 3D ("we", "our", or "the Game") is an arcade 3D fighting game for Android and Web. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you play the Game.

1. Information We Collect

We do not directly collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email, phone number, or contacts). All game progress, custom AI fighters, and settings are stored locally on your device.

2. Google AdMob & AdSense Advertising

The Game uses Google AdMob and Google AdSense to display banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded video advertisements. Google may use your device's Advertising ID (AAID) and coarse location/diagnostics data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads and prevent fraud.

Learn more about how Google uses data from partner apps at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

3. Children's Privacy

The Game is rated Teen (13+) for arcade martial arts fantasy violence and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

4. Contact Us

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, contact the developer via the support email listed on the Google Play Store page.
